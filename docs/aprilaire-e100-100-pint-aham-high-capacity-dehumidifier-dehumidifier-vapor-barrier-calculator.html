Why do big-box residential dehumidifiers freeze up in a crawl space?

Crawl spaces and basements stay around 52°F–62°F year-round. Cheap residential dehumidifiers lack hot-gas bypass defrost valves, so their evaporator coils ice over solid below 65°F. Commercial crawl space units (AprilAire E100 (100 Pint AHAM) High-Capacity Dehumidifier) use low-grain refrigerant (LGR) or hot-gas defrost to operate down to 36°F.
